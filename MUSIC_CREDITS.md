# Music Credits

This app uses local storage to play audio files.
To test this app, you should place your own `.mp3` files in the device storage (e.g., Download folder or Music folder on the emulator).

If you want to bundle sample audio files with the app instead:
1. Download small licensed sample audio files (e.g., from Free Music Archive, Incompetech, or Bensound).
2. Ensure the total size is <10MB.
3. Place the `.mp3` files in the `assets/audio/sample_songs/` directory.
4. The files will then be loaded by the application.

## Sources for Free Licensed Music
- **Free Music Archive**: https://freemusicarchive.org/
- **Incompetech**: https://incompetech.com/music/
- **Bensound**: https://www.bensound.com/
